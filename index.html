No. 003 — What's on the bill

Bill creationAI-READ
·Photo → itemized list — Gemini vision reads names, quantities, and prices straight off the receipt.
·Paste from chat — drop a messy group-chat order list, it parses into items.
·Tax, service, delivery, tip, discount — with one-tap 10/15/20% presets.
·Auto-capitalized, auto-formatted — names and prices clean themselves up as you type.
Splitting & claimingFAIR SHARE
·By item, or split evenly — toggle per bill, remembered next time.
·Shared items split automatically — one plate, three forks, three even shares.
·Down to the centavo — rounding always reconciles to the receipt's real total.
·Round to the nearest ₱1/5/10 — optional, for easier cash change.
PaymentsGET PAID
·GCash, cash, or other — with reference numbers and notes.
·Partial payments — pay some now, see what's still owed.
·One-tap "mark paid" — for cash handed over in person.
·Reminders that write themselves — pre-filled with the amount and GCash info.
The groupNO SIGN-UP
·Join by QR or link — no account, no app install.
·Add a guest who's not on the app — you manage their share for them.
·Colored avatars, item counts — see who ordered what at a glance.
Built to actually workRELIABLE
·Keeps working with no signal — edits queue and sync when you're back online.
·Undo, not "are you sure?" — deletes are instant and reversible for a few seconds.
·Installable, live-updating — add to home screen; everyone's screen updates in real time.
